# AOCS / FACSAT Technical Portfolio

Technical preparation and portfolio work focused on spacecraft Attitude and Orbit Control Systems (AOCS), simulation, and control-system engineering.

## Focus areas

- Spacecraft rotational dynamics
- Attitude states and angular velocity
- Magnetorquers and magnetometer interaction
- Sensor/actuator reasoning
- MATLAB/Simulink modeling
- Control-system validation
- Monte Carlo acceptance thinking
- FMEA / FDIR concepts
- GNC fundamentals
- Technical communication and visualization

## Why this project matters

This work extends my control-systems background into aerospace applications and demonstrates my ability to learn a new engineering domain, organize technical requirements, model system behavior, and communicate complex concepts clearly.
