# Biomedical Instrumentation & Additive Manufacturing

Undergraduate research project focused on designing and manufacturing electrode systems for electrical stimulation and impedance measurement in cell cultures.

## Project scope

**Institution:** Universidad Autónoma de Occidente  
**Tools:** SolidWorks, SLA additive manufacturing, experimental testing

## Contributions

- Designed, manufactured, and tested **62 electrode devices** for electrostimulation and electrical-impedance measurement in cell-culture applications.
- Iterated mechanical designs in SolidWorks and fabricated prototypes using resin-based 3D printing.
- Performed experimental validation and evaluated electrical impedance behavior.
- Reached approximately **10 Ω impedance** in the developed configuration.
- Presented the work as an oral contribution at **CIIMA 2025 — XIV International Congress of Mechatronics Engineering and Automation** in Medellín, Colombia.
