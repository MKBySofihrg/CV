# Control Systems & PLC Projects

Academic engineering projects focused on modeling, system identification, feedback control, state-space methods, and sequential automation.

## DC Servomotor — Modeling and PID Control

**Tools:** MATLAB, Simulink, System Identification

- Derived and validated a mathematical model of a DC servomotor using open-loop experimentation.
- Applied system-identification techniques to compare experimental and theoretical behavior.
- Achieved a **99.02% model fit** relative to the theoretical model.
- Designed and evaluated PID control behavior in simulation.

### Skills demonstrated

System modeling, experimental validation, identification, feedback control, MATLAB/Simulink, quantitative analysis.

## Magnetic Levitation — State-Space Modeling & Multi-Controller Design

**Tools:** MATLAB, Simulink, state-space analysis

- Modeled nonlinear magnetic-levitation dynamics and linearized the system around an operating point.
- Derived a state-space representation and transfer function.
- Designed and compared pole-placement, observer-based, and discrete controllers.
- Targeted approximately **5 s settling time** and **10% overshoot** design requirements.

### Skills demonstrated

State-space modeling, control design, observers, discretization, requirements-driven engineering.

## PLC Packaging Line — Sequential Automation

**Tools:** PLC, Ladder, Grafcet, Gemma

- Designed sequential control logic for an automated packaging line.
- Coordinated **5 actuators and 4 presence sensors**.
- Implemented Automatic, Cycle-by-cycle, Step-by-step, and Manual operating modes.
- Included emergency stop behavior and fault-recovery logic.

### Skills demonstrated

PLC programming, industrial automation logic, state-based behavior, sensors/actuators, safety-oriented thinking.
